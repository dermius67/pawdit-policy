Privacy Policy for Pawdit

Effective Date: September 25, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

Pawdit: Cat Expense Tracker ("Pawdit", "we", "us", or "our") is designed from the ground up on a foundation of absolute personal privacy and data sovereignty. We believe your daily financial transactions and behavioral habits are your business alone.

1. Zero Remote Data Collection

Pawdit operates 100% offline on your mobile device:

  • No Accounts Required: You do not need an account, email address, phone number, or login to use Pawdit.
  • No Cloud Servers: We operate zero remote databases, backend APIs, or web servers that receive your financial entries.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, crash trackers, advertising identifiers, or user telemetry libraries.

2. Local Storage on Device

All data you enter into Pawdit—including logged transactions, notes, categories, salary calibrations, Cat Math calculations, 9-Lives count, Goldfish Treats balance, and room decor progression—is saved strictly on your device using local operating system storage (such as SharedPreferences and local databases). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

3. Data Export & Portability

Pawdit provides two user-initiated data portability features:

  • CSV Audit Ledger: Allows you to copy your logged transactions in standard spreadsheet format to your system clipboard for import into spreadsheet applications.
  • Data Vault JSON Backup: Allows you to copy a structured text payload of your entire app state to the clipboard for manual archiving or transfer to another device.

Both export actions are executed completely locally. We never receive or process your exported text.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Pawdit offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Pawdit Pro Lifetime Unlock") processed securely through Google Play Billing. Transactions are handled exclusively by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. Pawdit does not collect, receive, or store your credit card information or banking details.

5. Children's Privacy

Pawdit is designated for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. Because Pawdit does not collect your contact details, we encourage you to review this page periodically.
